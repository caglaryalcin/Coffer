# Coffer brand assets

These files reuse the exact mark from `public/favicon-v2.svg`.

## Files

- `coffer-mark.svg`, `coffer-mark-512.png`, and `coffer-mark-192.png`: square product mark.
- `coffer-apple-touch-icon-180.png`: Apple touch icon export.
- `coffer-favicon-16.png` and `coffer-favicon-32.png`: small browser exports.
- `coffer-wordmark-on-light.svg` and `.png`: dark wordmark for light surfaces.
- `coffer-wordmark-on-dark.svg` and `.png`: light wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `coffer-brand-sheet.png`: visual reference sheet for the complete set.
- `coffer-og-1200x630.svg`: canonical source for the social preview at `public/og.png`.

## Core colors

- Soft red: `#CA7373`
- Ink: `#181A1B`
- Warm white: `#F7F5F0`
- Warm paper (social preview): `#F4F3EE`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects, or place the dark wordmark on a low-contrast surface.
